# Bringing "parallel" into the logo

The logo (`man/figures/logo.png`, drawn by `man/figures/logo.R`) shows the
parvenu. To say something about the *par* in parvenu, parallel work on all
cores, it now has parallel lines in two places.

## Done

- **Pinstripes.** Thin gold lines on the dark green coat, all at one slant
  and 20 units apart, at 45% opacity. They are clipped to the coat
  triangles, so they run unbroken across every facet: the coat has one
  shared direction while the face's lines run every which way.
- **Hat band as two stripes.** The green band of the bowler is split into
  two thin parallel stripes. Too small to carry the idea alone, but it
  echoes the pinstripes.

## Related changes

- **Text at the sides.** "par" sits left of the face, between the eyes and
  the tip of the nose; "venu" sits right, between the ear and the collar.
  This frees the coat so the pinstripes stay unbroken, and fills the empty
  background beside the face. The text keeps its pale gold with a dark
  green outline (`stroke-width="6"`): a little showy, which suits a
  parvenu.
- **Closed face.** The face used to have a few triangles knocked out of
  place, cubist style, which let green background show through as lines
  across the face. Those shifts are gone, so the face is now a coherent
  low-poly surface with a clean outline.

## Dropped

- **Parallel gaps in the face** (moving every knocked-out piece the same
  way). Moot now that the face has no gaps, and invisible at 139 px anyway.
- **Echoes of the profile** (faint shifted copies of his outline). The
  strongest "parallel" signal, but it would clutter the logo at small
  size and add lines to the background, which should stay plain.
- **Filling the gaps with a darker skin tone.** Tried as an alternative to
  closing the face. The tan lines looked like scars.
